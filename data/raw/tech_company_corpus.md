# Tech Company Corpus

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence company headquartered in San Francisco. OpenAI was founded in 2015 by Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever, John Schulman, Wojciech Zaremba, and Elon Musk.

Microsoft invested in OpenAI and provides cloud infrastructure for OpenAI through Azure. Azure is Microsoft's cloud computing platform.

OpenAI created ChatGPT as a conversational AI product. OpenAI also developed GPT-4 as a large language model for reasoning and generation.

Sam Altman is the chief executive officer of OpenAI. Greg Brockman is a co-founder of OpenAI and has served as president of OpenAI.

Microsoft is a technology company headquartered in Redmond, Washington. Microsoft was founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen.

Microsoft created Azure, Windows, and Microsoft Office. Microsoft acquired LinkedIn in 2016 and acquired GitHub in 2018.

GitHub is a software development platform headquartered in San Francisco. GitHub is a subsidiary of Microsoft.

LinkedIn is a professional networking platform headquartered in Sunnyvale, California. LinkedIn is a subsidiary of Microsoft.

Google is a technology company headquartered in Mountain View, California. Google was founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin.

Alphabet is the parent company of Google. Google created Google Search, Gmail, Android, and Google Cloud.

Google acquired DeepMind in 2014. DeepMind is an artificial intelligence research company headquartered in London.

DeepMind developed AlphaGo and AlphaFold. AlphaFold is a product and research system for protein structure prediction.

Google Cloud is a cloud computing platform developed by Google. Google Cloud competes with Microsoft Azure and Amazon Web Services.

Meta is a social technology company headquartered in Menlo Park, California. Meta was founded as Facebook in 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg, Eduardo Saverin, Andrew McCollum, Dustin Moskovitz, and Chris Hughes.

Meta acquired Instagram in 2012. Instagram is a social media product focused on photo and video sharing.

Meta acquired WhatsApp in 2014. WhatsApp is a messaging product used for consumer communication.

Mark Zuckerberg is the chief executive officer of Meta. Meta created Facebook and develops AI systems for social media and advertising.

Apple is a technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California. Apple was founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne.

Apple created the iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. Apple acquired Beats Electronics in 2014.

Anthropic is an artificial intelligence company headquartered in San Francisco. Anthropic was founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei, and other former OpenAI employees.

Anthropic created Claude as an AI assistant product. Amazon invested in Anthropic and provides cloud infrastructure for Anthropic through Amazon Web Services.

Amazon is a technology and e-commerce company headquartered in Seattle, Washington. Amazon was founded in 1994 by Jeff Bezos.

Amazon created Amazon Web Services as a cloud computing platform. Amazon acquired Twitch in 2014 and acquired Whole Foods Market in 2017.

NVIDIA is a semiconductor company headquartered in Santa Clara, California. NVIDIA was founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem.

NVIDIA created CUDA and develops GPUs used for artificial intelligence workloads. NVIDIA partners with cloud providers including Microsoft, Google, and Amazon.

Tesla is an electric vehicle and energy company headquartered in Austin, Texas. Tesla was founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning.

Elon Musk invested in Tesla in 2004 and later became chief executive officer of Tesla. Tesla created the Model S, Model 3, Model X, and Model Y.

Elon Musk co-founded OpenAI and later led Tesla. This connects OpenAI and Tesla through a shared person entity.

Microsoft partners with NVIDIA to provide GPU infrastructure for AI workloads on Azure. Amazon partners with NVIDIA to provide GPU infrastructure for AI workloads on Amazon Web Services.

Google partners with NVIDIA for AI infrastructure and GPU acceleration on Google Cloud. These cloud partnerships connect NVIDIA with Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, and Amazon Web Services.
